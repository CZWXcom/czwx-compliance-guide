# CZWX Compliance Guide: How We Verify Suppliers and Factories

CZWX (Quanzhou Haoyan Trading Co., Ltd.) is a cross-border retailer and distributor of licensed IP merchandise. This guide describes, in general terms, the checks we run before we list or sell a product. It names no supplier and no factory, and it publishes no audit document — those are confidential and are shared only with the parties that need them.

## 1. Who we are

- A trading company registered in China (founded 2016), based in Jinjiang, Fujian.
- We buy licensed merchandise from authorized suppliers and resell it to retailers, distributors and e-commerce sellers.
- We also work with factories in Dongguan and Quanzhou, China, developing customised and exclusive product lines with partners.

## 2. What we sell

Licensed IP merchandise: toys, gifts, homeware, stationery, apparel and accessories featuring popular entertainment brands. Our core rule is simple:

> **No authorization documentation, no listing.** A product only goes on sale when the paperwork backing it is in place.

## 3. Supplier verification

Before onboarding a supplier, we check:

1. **Business registration** — a valid business licence and a verifiable company identity.
2. **Authorization documentation for each brand** — collected as a set before the first order:
   - a distributor authorization letter naming the brand, product scope, authorized territory, sales channels and validity period;
   - proof of the supplier's own upstream authorization (ideally the brand-to-supplier authorization; otherwise a supplier-issued authorized-distributor confirmation listing brand and territory);
   - a brand product list matching the authorization.
3. **Territory and channel checks** — authorization is verified per brand, per region and per sales channel. A product authorized for one market is not assumed to be authorized for another.
4. **Platform requirements** — where a sales channel (for example a marketplace) requires its own brand registration, we confirm what that channel accepts before listing.

Generic, brand-wide authorization letters without brand, territory or validity details are not accepted as proof.

## 4. Factory audit standards

Where products are custom-made, we ask the factory for:

- **Factory profile** — full legal name and address, year founded, main products and processes, production capacity, main customers and export markets.
- **Audit credentials and recent audit reports** — covering the standards relevant to the product, including FAMA (Disney), BSCI, SMETA/Sedex and ISO certifications and the most recent audit report.
- **Compliance requirements for licensed artwork** — whether the factory requires the customer to provide authorization documents before producing licensed designs.

We verify these documents ourselves. We do not publish factory names, locations, audit reports or scores, and we do not disclose a factory's identity to downstream customers.

## 5. Product listing rules

- Only products backed by authorization documentation are listed.
- Brand and product names are not altered; prices follow the supplier's recommended retail price where required; regional restrictions are checked per product.
- Authorization expiry dates are tracked, and documentation is re-verified before reorders.
- Product images are used only where the supplier's terms allow trade customers to use them.

## 6. Ongoing monitoring

Verification is not a one-off. We re-check documentation when an authorization approaches expiry, when a supplier changes its terms, or when a new sales channel or territory is added.
