# Procurement Checklist: 10 Checks Before You Buy Licensed Merchandise

A generic checklist any buyer can use when vetting a supplier of licensed IP merchandise — whether or not you buy from CZWX.

1. **Is the supplier a real, registered company?** Ask for the business licence and verify the legal name, address and registration number.
2. **Does the authorization name the brand?** A letter that says "licensed products" without naming the brand is not proof for any specific brand.
3. **Does it cover your product?** Check product scope — the authorization must cover the product type you are buying.
4. **Does it cover your territory?** Authorization is per region. Confirm your market is included in writing.
5. **Does it cover your channel?** A letter for wholesale may not cover a marketplace, your own website, or another platform. Confirm each channel.
6. **Is it still valid?** Check the expiry date. An expired authorization is not an authorization.
7. **Can the supplier prove its own upstream authorization?** Ask how the supplier itself is authorized by the brand — ideally the brand-to-supplier authorization, otherwise a supplier-issued confirmation listing brand and territory.
8. **Does the product list match?** Compare the products offered against the authorized brand product list. Anything outside the list needs separate confirmation.
9. **If the product is custom-made, has the factory been audited?** Ask for the factory's audit credentials and recent audit reports (for example FAMA, BSCI, SMETA/Sedex, ISO) — and verify them rather than taking a claim at face value.
10. **Is the paperwork consistent?** The company name on the authorization, the invoice and the business licence should be the same legal entity. If a name differs (for example a missing word), ask for the licence and a written explanation before paying.

*This checklist is general guidance, not legal advice. Confirm requirements with the brand owner, the supplier and the sales channel in writing before ordering.*
