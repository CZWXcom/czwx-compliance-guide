# CZWX Supplier & Factory Compliance Guide

**CZWX — Quanzhou Haoyan Trading Co., Ltd.** is a cross-border retailer and distributor of licensed IP merchandise, based in Jinjiang, Fujian, China. We supply licensed toys, gifts, homeware, stationery, apparel and accessories to retailers, distributors and e-commerce sellers in the EU, UK, North America and other markets.

- Website: https://www.czwx.com
- Wholesale enquiries: wholesale@czwx.com

## What this repository is

This repository publishes, in general terms, the compliance process CZWX follows before a product is listed or sold. It is written for buyers, retail partners and anyone who wants to know how we check the suppliers and factories behind the products we distribute.

For a practical example, see [CZWX's buyer checklist for licensed character merchandise](https://czwx.com/en/news/character-product-document-checklist-en/), which covers products, territories, sales channels and authorised parties.

## Contents

- [COMPLIANCE-GUIDE.md](COMPLIANCE-GUIDE.md) — How we verify suppliers and factories, what authorization documentation we collect, and the rules we apply before listing a product.
- [PROCUREMENT-CHECKLIST.md](PROCUREMENT-CHECKLIST.md) — A 10-point checklist any buyer can use when vetting a supplier, whether or not they buy from CZWX.

## What this repository is not

- It is not a legal certification, and it is not a substitute for a buyer's own due diligence.
- It does not name any supplier or factory, and it does not publish any audit report, certificate, price or commercial term. Factory identities and audit documents are confidential and are shared only with the parties that need them, under the relevant agreements.
- Product availability, pricing and sales territories are confirmed per order with the supplier in writing.
